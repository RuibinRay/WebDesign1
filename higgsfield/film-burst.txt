One continuous shot with no cuts, filmed in extreme slow motion as if at 1000 frames per second. A soda can stands upright in a few centimeters of clear water at the edge of a tropical beach at midday. The can never moves and its label stays sharp and exactly as it is in the first frame.

A small clear wave closes around the base of the can and rises into a tall crown-shaped splash. Fresh tropical fruit erupts out of the water with it: pineapple wedges, mango cubes, halved passion fruit, shards of white coconut and a few roasted coffee beans. The fruit spreads outward to the left and to the right of the can and never passes in front of the label. By the end of the shot the fruit and hundreds of glassy droplets hang almost weightless in the air around the can, turning slowly and sparkling in the hard tropical sun.

Low camera at can height, 50mm lens, deep focus, locked off on a tripod with a barely perceptible push-in. Photoreal premium beverage commercial, high-speed photography, crisp frozen droplets, minimal motion blur so every frame is a sharp still. No people, no hands, no extra cans, no captions, no added text, no changes to the label.
