One continuous macro shot with no cuts, filmed in extreme slow motion as if at 1000 frames per second. The top of a cold aluminum soda can at golden hour, beaded with condensation, with the sea behind it dissolved into warm orange and turquoise bokeh. The can stays in the center of the frame.

The pull tab lifts by itself and the lid cracks open with a snap. A fine cold mist bursts upward, then a fountain of tiny sparkling droplets and bubbles rises from the opening, backlit by the low sun so every droplet glows gold. Pale fizz foams gently around the opening and a single drop runs across the lid.

100mm macro lens, shallow depth of field, focus locked on the opening, very slow push-in, no shake. Photoreal premium beverage commercial, high-speed macro photography, crisp frozen droplets, minimal motion blur so every frame is a sharp still. No hands, no fingers, no people, no text, no lettering, no logos, no dark or colored liquid.
