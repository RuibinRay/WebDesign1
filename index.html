    Inside the can

    Nothing to hide under the lid.

    We started with a blender, a cooler and a rule: if it does not grow near the beach, it does not go in. That is still the whole recipe.

    • Real fruit juice. Pressed, not flavored.
    • Coconut water for body instead of syrup.
    • A pinch of Pacific sea salt. It makes the fruit louder.
    • Gentle bubbles. Closer to sea foam than to soda pop.
